Tailor-Made Operations Without Multi-Million Dollar Bloat

Legacy tier-1 ERP platforms (SAP, NetSuite, Oracle) present mid-market manufacturing, logistics, and field service companies with insurmountable barriers: seven-figure implementation quotes, rigid data structures that require changing your core business workflows, and endless consulting retainers.

By architecting custom ERP applications on Zoho Creator, you obtain enterprise-grade data modeling, automated work order routing, multi-tier bill of materials calculations, and real-time inventory adjustments customized exactly to your proprietary operational workflow—at a fraction of legacy cost.

Core ERP Engineering Modules

Bespoke modules engineered within custom Zoho Creator ERP systems:

01

Multi-Warehouse Inventory & Serial Tracking

Track raw materials, assemblies, and finished goods across multi-location warehouses with automated reorder triggers, barcode scanning, and FIFO inventory valuation.

02

Bill of Materials (BOM) & Shop Floor Routing

Automate multi-level BOM explosion, machine-hour allocation, scrap tracking, and stage-by-stage work order routing from raw materials intake to QA sign-off.

03

Procurement & Supplier Performance Portals

Streamline purchase requisitions, RFQ bidding matrices, purchase order dispatch, supplier delivery SLAs, and automatic receipt quality checks.

04

Logistics Dispatch & Real-Time Traceability

Connect packing slip generation, carrier dispatch webhooks (DHL, FedEx, UPS), container tracking, and customer tracking portals in a unified interface.

5-Stage ERP Engineering Protocol

Structured implementation methodology guaranteeing seamless shop floor adoption:

01 Operations Audit

Mapping existing floor workflows, stockroom routes, BOM dependencies, supplier channels, and accounting tie-ins.

02 Relational ERD

Architecting normalized schema tables for items, assemblies, warehouses, serial numbers, suppliers, and purchase orders.

03 Core Engine

Authoring Deluge business logic for auto-reserve inventory, work order batch progression, and dynamic cost rollups.

04 Pilot Testing

Testing real production runs, mock inventory variances, supplier portal submissions, and financial ledger sync.

05 Plant Cutover

Shop floor device configuration, verified inventory count intake, role provisioning, and step-by-step video training.
