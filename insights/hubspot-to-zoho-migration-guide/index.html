How to Migrate from HubSpot to Zoho CRM with Zero Data Loss

As HubSpot contact tiers and marketing hubs increase in price, scaling businesses migrate to Zoho CRM for superior customization, transparent licensing, and the unified Zoho One ecosystem. Here is the complete migration roadmap for administrators.

1. Preparation & Pre-Migration Data Audit

The biggest mistake in CRM migration is importing uncleaned legacy data. Before exporting from HubSpot:

  • Deduplicate Contacts: Run HubSpot's native deduplication tool on Email and Phone numbers.
  • Archive Inactive Contacts: Filter out bounced emails, unsubscribes, and contacts with no activity in 2+ years into a separate cold archive file.
  • Standardize Picklists: Ensure country codes, state names, and lead source picklist values in HubSpot match Zoho CRM's standard picklists exactly.

2. Schema & Object Mapping Matrix

HubSpot and Zoho CRM organize relational objects with slight structural differences:

HubSpot Object Zoho CRM Equivalent Key Mapping Considerations
Companies Accounts Match on Company Domain Name & Company ID.
Contacts Contacts / Leads Map lifecycle stage: Subscribers/MQLs to Leads, SQLs/Customers to Contacts.
Deals Deals (Potentials) Map pipeline stages, probability percentages, and expected close dates.
Engagements / Notes Notes & Closed Activities Preserve timestamp and author mapping via historic audit fields.

3. The 5-Step Ingestion Sequence (Strict Hierarchy)

To maintain relational links without broken orphan records, data must be imported in a strict hierarchical order:

Step 1

Users & Roles Ingestion

Create all team users, roles, and profiles in Zoho CRM first so records can be assigned to their rightful owners during import.

Step 2

Accounts (Companies) Import

Import Companies first. Zoho CRM will generate unique internal Account IDs that will serve as parent keys for contacts and deals.

Step 3

Contacts Import & Parent Linking

Import Contacts, using Company Name or HubSpot Company ID as the lookup anchor to automatically attach contacts to their parent Accounts.

Step 4

Deals (Pipelines) Import

Import Deals linked simultaneously to the parent Account and primary Contact, ensuring pipeline stage mapping is 100% aligned.

Step 5

Historical Notes, Calls & Attachments

Import call logs, meeting summaries, and historic notes attached directly to their respective Contact and Deal IDs.

4. Preserving Historical Notes & Call Logs

Sales reps rely heavily on historic interaction context. When extracting HubSpot engagements, ensure you preserve the Created Date and Author Email. In Zoho CRM, enable "Audit Field Permissions" in the administrator security settings to allow populating historic creation timestamps during migration.

5. The Live Cutover & Post-Launch Checklist

ADMINISTRATOR CUTOVER PROTOCOL

1. Freeze write access in HubSpot on Friday evening.
2. Run final differential export of records created in the last 48 hours.
3. Import differential data into Zoho CRM sandbox and validate sample records.
4. Switch DNS / web form action URLs to Zoho webhooks on Saturday.
5. Conduct user onboarding and go-live on Monday morning.
