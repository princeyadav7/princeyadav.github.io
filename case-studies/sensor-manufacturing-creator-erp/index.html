01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

The client is a long-established industrial sensor and measurement equipment manufacturer with approximately 75 years of operating history. The organization designs, engineers, and manufactures highly specialized precision sensors, transducers, and industrial instrumentation supplied to commercial manufacturing plants, research institutions, and process industries globally.

The company operated across two distinct employee tiers: an administrative, engineering, and sales workforce of approximately 120 white-collar professionals, and a factory workforce of approximately 200 blue-collar machine operators, assemblers, and technicians. Modernizing operational processes across such an established business required addressing complex manufacturing execution, field servicing, and multi-channel commercial sales without disrupting daily factory floor operations.

To solve these challenges, we delivered a comprehensive digital transformation initiative connecting Zoho One, Zoho ERP, and Zoho FSM, augmented by a custom Zoho Creator application designed specifically for shop-floor blue-collar workforce job tracking.

Team Leadership & Engineering Role: As Senior Zoho Developer, Team Lead, and Solution Designer, I led the technical architecture and delivery across developer squads, translating complex shop-floor manufacturing workflows into an interconnected cloud ecosystem.
02 / Bottlenecks

Problem Faced

2.1 Disconnected Departmental Silos Across a 75-Year Manufacturing Legacy

With decades of established manufacturing routines, departments operated in disconnected informational silos across spreadsheets, paper work logs, and localized desktop records. Sales teams had no visibility into active factory lead times, while production managers were blind to incoming deal pipelines.

2.2 Multi-Source Inbound Lead Routing Bottlenecks

Enquiries arrived through web forms, digital advertisements, telephony, and WhatsApp. Sales leads were manually evaluated, causing delays in matching specialized sensor inquiries with the appropriate technical sales engineering groups.

2.3 Accessible Job Tracking for ~200 Blue-Collar Factory Workers

Approximately 200 blue-collar factory employees performed component assembly, calibration, and machining. The business lacked a simple, accessible mechanism to record daily or hourly completed job work without complex desktop software that workers found difficult to navigate.

2.4 Uncoordinated On-Site Demos, Calibration & Field Support

Technical industrial sensors require customer-site product demonstrations, calibration, installation, and scheduled maintenance. Field technicians operated without structured scheduling, digital checklists, or automated inventory handoffs from the central warehouse.

2.5 Dual-Mode Make-to-Stock (MTS) & Make-to-Order (MTO) Scheduling

The factory needed to balance standard catalogue inventory replenishment (Make-to-Stock) with bespoke, engineer-to-order customer projects (Make-to-Order) sharing common raw materials, CNC machining centres, and testing benches.

2.6 Paper-Based Shop-Floor Progress, Scrap & Waste Invisibility

Production progress, work-centre cycle times, and manufacturing waste were logged on disparate paper sheets, preventing production group heads from providing timely operational reports or scrap variance metrics to executive leadership.

Pillar 01 Commercial Operations

Zoho One: Sales, Workforce & Business Process Automation

Multi-Source Inbound Lead Management: Automated capture of technical sensor inquiries from web forms, digital marketing channels, telephone inquiries, and WhatsApp messaging into Zoho CRM.
CRM Blueprints & Standardized Sales Execution: Enforced mandatory qualification stages, technical datasheet attachments, and quote approval gates before deals progress to order confirmation.
Matrix-Based Territory & Product Assignment: Dynamically routed incoming leads based on customer geographic territory, product complexity (e.g., pressure vs. temperature sensors), and sales engineer technical specialization.
Automated ERP & Zoho Books Synchronization: Confirmed sales orders automatically generate draft invoices in Zoho Books and trigger manufacturing work order allocations in Zoho ERP without re-keying data.
Pillar 02 Shop-Floor Technology

Custom Zoho Creator Application for Blue-Collar Workforce

To eliminate paper work logs across approximately 200 blue-collar factory employees, we engineered a dedicated custom application on Zoho Creator featuring simplified touch-kiosk interfaces and mobile logging workflows:

Touch-Screen Kiosk & Mobile Interfaces: Designed simplified visual forms with large touch targets, minimal text entry, and barcode scanning for non-desk factory workers.
Daily & Hourly Completed Job Work Logging: Machine operators record assembled units, active work-centre hours, and batch completion statuses directly from the factory floor.
Shift Reconciliation & Supervisor Approvals: Shift supervisors review and authorize completed job batches before data reconciles with central ERP production orders.
Workforce Group Performance Tracking: Aggregates individual work logs into team productivity metrics, supporting payroll calculations and performance evaluations.
Pillar 03 Field Operations

Zoho FSM: Product Demonstrations, Installation & Maintenance

On-Site Product Demonstrations & Trial Tracking: Structured FSM service requests dispatch trial sensor kits, schedule pre-sales field engineers, and record trial outcomes directly into the CRM pipeline.
Sensor Installation & Calibration Checklists: Mandatory digital inspection checklists ensure technicians verify electrical parameters, mounting torques, and signal integrity prior to client sign-off.
Scheduled Maintenance & Warranty Servicing: Tracks installed base assets, automated calibration intervals, warranty coverage statuses, and field parts replacement.
Mobile Technician Workflow & Digital Signatures: Field specialists capture on-site photographs, diagnostic readings, and customer e-signatures on mobile tablets for immediate dispatch closure.
Pillar 04 Manufacturing Execution

Zoho ERP for Manufacturing Operations & Shop Floor Logistics

Dual Manufacturing Modes (MTS & MTO): Configured Zoho ERP to manage standard catalogue inventory replenishment alongside custom engineer-to-order customer projects sharing common raw materials.
Multi-Level Bills of Materials (BOM) & Routing: Detailed BOMs for sensor sub-assemblies (MEMS chips, housings, potting resins, cables) mapped across specialized factory work-centres.
Raw Material Inventory & Automated Reordering: Real-time inventory deduction during work order execution with automated purchase requisitions for critical low-stock components.
Shop-Floor Waste & Scrap Recording: Captures production scrap and material variances during manufacturing runs, generating automated scrap loss analytics for executive leadership.

05 / Systems Architecture

Technical Architecture & System Flow

The conceptual systems architecture illustrates how commercial sales, field service operations, and shop-floor manufacturing execution connect across Zoho One, Zoho ERP, Zoho FSM, and custom Zoho Creator applications:

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE • ZOHO ONE + ZOHO FSM + ZOHO ERP
Connected Operations

Architecture Data Flow Overview: The enterprise manufacturing architecture operates across three interconnected operational tiers. In Layer 1, commercial inquiries entering from digital web portals, telephony, and WhatsApp are captured in Zoho CRM, guided through strict Blueprint qualification gates, and mapped directly to specialized technical sales groups. Concurrently, customer-site sensor trials, calibration requests, and installation visits are dispatched through Zoho FSM with digital checklists.

In Layer 2, confirmed commercial sales orders and trial requirements flow directly into Zoho ERP to drive shop-floor execution. The system coordinates parallel Make-to-Stock (MTS) catalogue replenishments with Make-to-Order (MTO) custom engineering projects across multi-level Bills of Materials (BOM) and specialized work-centres. Crucially, a bespoke Zoho Creator touch-screen application provides ~200 blue-collar factory employees with a simplified interface to log completed hourly job batches, machine operations, and production scrap without complex ERP overhead.

In Layer 3, completed shop-floor work orders trigger finished good releases, raw material stock deductions, and reorder point alerts in Zoho Inventory. Confirmed deliveries synchronize directly into Zoho Books for accounting reconciliation and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) tracking. Real-time factory floor metrics aggregate into Zoho Analytics, empowering executive leadership with live visibility into scrap variance rates, work-centre cycle times, and worker group productivity.
