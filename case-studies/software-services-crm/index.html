01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

A fast-scaling software services and IT consulting organization needed a modernized, unified infrastructure to support their rapid commercial expansion. Their commercial operations lacked a standardized flow from initial inbound inquiry capture to formal proposal issuance.

Simultaneously, corporate email was siloed in legacy Microsoft Outlook subscriptions with no direct CRM visibility, and employee onboarding, leave requests, and performance tracking were managed manually across disconnected spreadsheets.

02 / Bottlenecks

The Business & Operational Challenges

Unstructured Conversion

When converting leads into deals, customer companies and key decision makers were frequently skipped or duplicated, corrupting CRM relational integrity.

Manual CPQ & Quote Delays

Quotes were drafted manually in spreadsheets and Word docs, resulting in pricing discrepancies, approval delays, and zero visibility into profit margins.

Email Siloing & High Licensing

Maintaining separate Outlook mailboxes for 100 users created high recurring licensing costs and prevented synchronized email threading inside CRM records.

Manual Spreadsheet HR

Employee onboarding, leave balances, attendance logs, and performance evaluations were tracked manually, creating friction for HR and managers.

03 / Solution

Core Architectural Solution Pillars

Pillar 01 Pipeline & Relational Data

Structured Sales Journey & Automated 3-Way Relational Conversion

Automated 3-Way Conversion: Configured a structured conversion engine that simultaneously creates a verified Account (Company), primary Contact (Decision Maker), and linked Deal (Opportunity) with zero orphaned records.
Blueprint Stage Validation: Implemented mandatory stage progression gates (Discovery, Technical Scoping, Proposal Submitted, Negotiation, Closed Won) enforcing commercial data entry.
Pillar 02 CPQ & Quoting Engine

Dynamic Deal-Linked Quote Engine & Pricing Automation

1-Click Deal-Linked Quoting: Linked the Zoho CRM Quotes module directly to Deals, auto-populating billing terms, contact details, and software rate cards in 1 click.
Dynamic PDF Proposals: Engineered branded quote templates with automated discount matrices and dynamic commercial value synchronization.
Pillar 03 Mail & People Operations

100-User Zero-Downtime Outlook Cutover & Zoho People HRMS

Zero-Downtime Mail Migration: Cut over 100 enterprise mailboxes from Microsoft Outlook to Zoho Mail with DKIM/SPF/DMARC hardening and full CRM auto-threading.
End-to-End Zoho People HRMS: Automated employee onboarding checklists, leave balances, attendance tracking, and multi-tier manager appraisal cycles for 100+ team members.
