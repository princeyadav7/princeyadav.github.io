01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

The client is a prominent solar engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) enterprise executing high-value renewable energy contracts. Their portfolio spans multi-megawatt public sector infrastructure projects—including government contracts to install rooftop solar arrays across major railway stations—alongside high-volume commercial and residential installations across wide geographic regions.

Handling complex tender bidding, multi-channel inbound customer leads, dynamic engineering quotations, on-site civil and electrical installation tracking, and multi-stage financial billing required a seamless, interconnected Zoho One architecture.

02 / Bottlenecks

The Engineering & Operational Challenges

Fragmented Inbound Ingestion

Inquiries arrived across website chatbots, WhatsApp, calls, and tender portals with no centralized technical scoring for kWp capacity or roof feasibility.

Sales-to-Site Disconnect

Handing won contracts over to field engineers was manual, causing lost site survey parameters, delayed BOM procurement, and uncoordinated installations.

Staggered Milestone Billing

Solar installations require staged invoicing (advance, dispatch, mounting, net metering). Disconnected accounts delayed cash collections and PO releases.

Job-Site Visibility Blind Spots

Operations managers supervising concurrent installations across railway stations and commercial sites lacked real-time task and inspection updates.

03 / Solution

Core Architectural Solution Pillars

Pillar 01 Lead Intake & CPQ

Omni-Channel Lead Routing & Solar Engineering Quotation Engine

Multi-Source Routing: Unified website chatbot leads (SalesIQ), WhatsApp API, Meta/Google ads, and tenders into Zoho CRM with kWp capacity scoring.
Engineering CPQ Engine: Built dynamic quotation templates calculating panel wattage, inverter sizing, mounting structures, and government subsidy deductions in real time.
Pillar 02 Operations & Field Delivery

Automated Deal-to-Projects Field Pipeline (5-Phase Framework)

Instant Project Workspace Spawning: Marking a Deal “Closed-Won” triggers Deluge to provision a templated Zoho Projects workspace linked to the CRM deal.
5-Phase Standardized EPC Milestones: Enforces sequential sign-offs across Site Feasibility, CAD Engineering Design, Material Dispatch, Inverter Wiring, and Government Net Metering.
Pillar 03 Finance & Project Accounting

Milestone-Based Invoicing & Job-Site Cost Accounting (Zoho Books)

Milestone Customer Invoicing: Completing project gates automatically triggers staged invoice generation in Zoho Books with direct payment link delivery.
Vendor Cost Attribution: Links module/inverter purchase orders and labor costs directly to the project cost center for real-time site gross margin calculation.
