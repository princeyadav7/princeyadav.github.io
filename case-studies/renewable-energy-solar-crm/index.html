01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

In commercial and government solar installation operations, business performance depends entirely on two deeply interdependent workflows: on-site project execution and specialized equipment procurement. A growing renewable energy enterprise in India executed complex Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) contracts ranging from 50 kW rooftop commercial installations to multi-megawatt ground-mounted government tenders.

While their field engineering teams required rigorous milestone management, task dependencies, and expense tracking across dozens of concurrent geographic locations, their supply chain team faced a severe tooling void. The organization operated under a Zoho One licensing model that did not include an enterprise procurement platform capable of handling multi-item purchase requests, multi-vendor Request for Quotation (RFQ) distribution, and granular side-by-side bid evaluations.

To solve this fundamental operational bottleneck, I served as the Zoho Systems Consultant and Solutions Architect. I designed and deployed a dual-engine architecture: implementing Zoho Projects to enforce structured multi-site installation management and site-level expense tracking, and architecting a bespoke Zoho Creator Procurement Application to automate purchase request approval gating, multi-channel vendor quotation sourcing, and automated matrix bid evaluation.

Consultant & Solutions Architect Scope: Because standard off-the-shelf procurement tools required expensive third-party licensing or lacked direct ties into project execution milestones, this implementation leveraged Zoho Creator to engineer a tailored procurement engine that cost-effectively resolved every supply chain bottleneck within the client’s existing Zoho One ecosystem.
02 / Bottlenecks

Problem Faced

2.1 Multiple Concurrent Installation Sites

Managing concurrent solar installations spread across disparate geographic regions created severe visibility blind spots. Senior executives and project leads lacked a centralized, real-time mechanism to track physical installation progress, contractor task assignments, and site readiness across active rooftop and ground-mounted facilities.

2.2 Strict Government Tender Project Requirements

Public sector and municipal tender contracts demanded strict documentation, precise milestone tracking, and organized execution records. Without standardized project management templates and auditable task logging, field engineers struggled to satisfy compliance prerequisites, jeopardizing contractual milestones and milestone-linked billing.

2.3 Project-Specific Financial & Expense Invisibility

Project managers lacked centralized oversight into project-specific invoices and incurred site expenses. Costs accumulated across field labour, civil foundation work, transport, and localized crane rentals without structured linkage to project IDs, making it impossible to calculate real-time project burn rates or prevent budget overrun.

2.4 Procurement Tooling Limitations Under Existing Licensing

The organization’s existing Zoho One subscription package did not include a procurement tool tailored for its technical equipment purchasing requirements. Commercial off-the-shelf procurement software required costly external software subscriptions and lacked native bidirectional data sync with their operational project records.

2.5 Uncontrolled Field Purchase Request Approval Gating

Field teams and project engineers required a formal, auditable process to raise material purchase requests that had to be systematically reviewed and authorized by designated project administrators before initiating external supplier RFQs. Unvetted requests frequently initiated duplicate vendor quotes or committed unapproved funds.

2.6 Fragmented & Delayed Vendor Outreach

Distributing quotation requests to equipment vendors via manual emails and phone calls caused inconsistent bidding documents, missing datasheets, uncoordinated response deadlines, and untracked communications. Critical solar components faced supply delays that stalled site installation crews.

2.7 Error-Prone Manual Quotation Comparison

When quotations arrived in varied formats (PDFs, spreadsheets, email text), administrators had to manually compile and compare unit rates, shipping terms, delivery lead times, and warranties across competing suppliers. This manual transcription was slow, highly prone to computational errors, and obscured the best commercial terms.

Pillar 02 Custom Procurement Engine

Custom Procurement Automation with Zoho Creator

Procuring high-value solar equipment—such as tier-1 monocrystalline panels, grid-tied string inverters, galvanized steel mounting structures, and transformer switchgear—requires a disciplined, multi-step procurement workflow. To eliminate the licensing gap in Zoho One, I developed a bespoke Zoho Creator application incorporating 8 core operational features:

Purchase Request Creation: Site engineers and project managers submit purchase requests in Zoho Creator itemizing required components, specifications, target quantities, and linking the request to the active Zoho Projects installation ID.
Administrator Approval Gate: Purchase requests are routed to the designated administrator for review. The approval acts as an authoritative gate: no external vendor RFQ can be triggered until technical specifications and budget allocations are validated.
RFQ Generation in Zoho Creator: Upon admin authorization, an official Request for Quotation (RFQ) record is automatically generated in Zoho Creator, pulling line-item specs, compliance criteria, delivery site address, and response deadlines.
Vendor Selection Workflow: The administrator selects approved equipment suppliers from the vendor registry to receive the specific RFQ based on component classification (e.g., Module Manufacturers, Inverter Specialists, BOS Suppliers).
Email & WhatsApp Distribution: The RFQ is automatically dispatched to selected vendors across both email and WhatsApp messaging, delivering bidding instructions and a secure web link for quotation submission simultaneously.
Online Quotation Submission: Suppliers access an external vendor quotation form to enter itemized pricing, delivery timelines, product warranties, tax schedules, and upload official proposal PDF documents without requiring system user accounts.
Automated Quotation Comparison: Submitted bids are compiled automatically into an intuitive side-by-side comparison matrix, mapping pricing, delivery schedules, and warranty terms across all competing suppliers for the active RFQ.
Admin Review & Award Authorization: The administrator evaluates the structured comparison table, conducts commercial reviews, and authorizes the approved quotation—automatically transitioning the RFQ to awarded status and notifying unselected bidders.
Pillar 03 Automated Quotation Comparison Matrix

Real-Time Multi-Vendor Evaluation & Bid Analysis Engine

The defining operational capability of the custom Creator application is its Automated Quotation Comparison Matrix. When multiple vendors submit bids against an active RFQ through external web forms, the application compiles their inputs into a structured comparative evaluation engine, completely replacing manual spreadsheets and uncoordinated PDF reviews.

Standardized Commercial Normalization: Automatically reconciles disparate supplier pricing formats into standardized unit rates, freight allowances, and statutory tax breakdowns for exact like-for-like financial appraisal.
Technical Compliance & Warranty Auditing: Cross-checks stated module performance degradation curves, inverter MTBF rates, and manufacturer defect warranty durations against tender specification thresholds.
Lead Time & Site Readiness Alignment: Deluge validation checks supplier delivery schedules against planned installation milestones in Zoho Projects, flagging proposals with transit risks that could cause site idle time.
Single-Click Award & PO Integration: Authorizing a winning proposal within the matrix automatically marks unselected bids as closed, generates a formal Purchase Order, and records committed expenditure against the Zoho Projects budget.
Pillar 04 Supporting Ecosystem

Supporting Zoho Applications: Analytics, SalesIQ & Marketing

Zoho Analytics — Executive Dashboards: Connected directly to Zoho Projects and the Zoho Creator RFQ application. Visualizes site completion velocities, procurement cycle times, vendor price variance trends, and budget vs. actual variance across all active tender installations.
Zoho SalesIQ — Live Web Engagement: Configured on the enterprise web portal to capture incoming commercial solar inquiries and tender partner contacts, routing leads with geographical metadata into project discovery pipelines.
Zoho Marketing Automation — Supplier & Client Communications: Set up automated campaign workflows to notify certified suppliers of upcoming tender procurement cycles, deliver vendor onboarding guides, and broadcast milestone completion bulletins to commercial clients.

05 / Systems Architecture

Technical Architecture & System Flow

The conceptual systems architecture illustrates how physical field execution in Zoho Projects integrates with custom low-code procurement automation in Zoho Creator, establishing a continuous data pipeline from initial purchase request through multi-vendor bidding, milestone completion, and executive reporting:

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE • ZOHO PROJECTS + CREATOR + ANALYTICS
Connected Operations

Architecture Data Flow Overview: The implementation organizes operations across three coordinated tiers. In Layer 1, field engineering and installation operations are orchestrated within Zoho Projects, managing commercial EPC contracts, government tender milestones, and mobile site logs alongside real-time expense and invoicing records.

In Layer 2, a custom Zoho Creator procurement engine resolves the licensing and functional gap in standard tooling. Field engineers raise itemized purchase requests tied to specific project IDs, which undergo strict administrator approval gating before generating automated RFQs. Bidding packages are distributed across email and WhatsApp messaging to registered vendors, who submit quotations through secure, tokenized external web forms into an automated comparison matrix.

In Layer 3, supporting intelligence and engagement applications unify organizational reporting. Zoho Analytics ingests live data across Projects and Creator to deliver executive visibility into project burn rates, procurement cycle times, and vendor price variances. Simultaneously, Zoho SalesIQ and Zoho Marketing Automation drive inbound commercial project acquisition and supplier network communications.
