01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

The client is a pioneering Outside Broadcast (OB) and live media production enterprise, renowned for engineering first-generation flypacks and deploying broadcast kits, production crews, and OB units for landmark sporting tournaments, concerts, and news events across 21+ countries spanning South Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.

With tens of thousands of high-value broadcast assets (Ultra-HD cameras, specialized lenses, production switchers, audio consoles, flypack containers, and kilometers of optical fiber) constantly moving between international warehouses, cargo freight, and stadium compounds, the organization required a centralized, real-time operational ERP to eliminate equipment loss, dispatch delays, and maintenance blind spots.

02 / Bottlenecks

The Operational & Logistics Challenges

High-Velocity Gear Movements

Broadcast kits deployed concurrently to multiple stadiums globally. Paper-based manifests caused discrepancies in tracking serialized lenses and camera bodies.

Warehouse Dispatch Bottlenecks

Loading massive freight containers lacked instant barcode/RFID verification, risking missing cables or converters that could jeopardize live broadcasts.

Maintenance & Repair (R&M) Gaps

Damaged gear returning from harsh weather was mixed into active stock without diagnostic testing logs, technician bench tickets, or vendor RMA tracking.

Unstructured Procurement Approvals

Purchase requisitions for event replacements lacked automated approval matrices, causing delays and unbudgeted emergency procurement spending.

03 / Solution

Core Architectural Solution Pillars

Pillar 01 Asset Master & RFID

Hierarchical Asset Master & Handheld RFID Bulk Scanning

Multi-Tier Asset Hierarchy: Engineered structured relationships (Flypack Container → Base Production Kit → Parent Electronic Unit → Modular Components) covering optics, RF, switchers, and audio.
Handheld RFID Scanning: Built mobile interfaces in Zoho Creator connecting to RFID readers for instant container check-out, verifying 100+ components in seconds during freight loading.
Pillar 02 Dispatch & Procurement

Digital Delivery Challans, Customs Gate Passes & Multi-Tier PR/PO Engine

Digital Delivery Challan Engine: Auto-generates customs-ready digital manifests with embedded QR verification, crew signatory capture, and gate-pass clearance.
Approval-Gated Procurement: Built conditional Purchase Requisition (PR) matrices that instantly transform approved requisitions into formal POs in Zoho Books.
Pillar 03 Maintenance & Multi-App Sync

R&M Maintenance Lifecycle & CRM/Books Bi-Directional Integration

Field Incident & Bench Testing Work Orders: Field engineers log equipment errors directly from stadium trucks; technicians record bench tests and spare-part consumption before release.
CRM & Books Synchronization: Winning a contract in CRM immediately locks gear allocation schedules in Creator, while POs and vendor bills sync into Books via REST APIs.
