01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

A fast-scaling international healthcare organization specializing in clinical rehabilitation and custom prosthetics manufacturing required a unified, connected operating system to orchestrate its multi-country patient care and medical delivery lifecycle. Operating clinical facilities and manufacturing centers across Japan, India, the Philippines, and other international territories, the organization needed to connect front-end patient intake with clinical verification, billing, inventory release, and last-mile device delivery.

Prior to this engagement, patient records, payment collections, and prosthetic components were managed across disparate departmental systems. Clinical teams required verified patient data before initiating custom manufacturing; finance needed automated billing and payment gateway reconciliations across multiple currencies; and warehouse teams needed accurate component tracking, automated reorder triggers, and logistics visibility to assemble and deliver custom-fitted prosthetics without scheduling delays.

To solve these challenges, we architected and deployed an enterprise-wide Zoho One application suite tailored to 200+ enterprise users across international clinical, financial, administrative, and supply-chain roles. By interconnecting Zoho CRM, Zoho Books, Zoho Inventory, Zoho Forms, Zoho People, Zoho Payroll, Zoho Recruit, Zoho Sign, Zoho Meeting, Zoho Campaigns, Zoho Analytics, Zoho Connect, and Zoho Learn, the organization unified patient care with reliable operational governance.

Implementation Scope: My mandate as lead Zoho consultant covered cross-border systems architecture, configuring 200+ user roles across international clinics, architecting the patient-to-fulfilment pipeline across CRM, Books, and Inventory, and implementing automated compliance and data-retention policies.
02 / Bottlenecks

Problem Faced

2.1 Managing 200+ Enterprise Users Across Clinical and Administrative Roles

Operating across Japan, India, the Philippines, and other regions, the company employed more than 200 personnel with distinct operational responsibilities—including healthcare intake coordinators, clinical specialists, prosthetics fitting technicians, billing accountants, and administrative directors. Each role required precisely scoped application access, regional data segregation, and strict profile permissions.

2.2 Sensitive Patient Information & Multi-Jurisdiction Data Privacy

Because the business handled confidential medical details, physiological measurements, and treatment histories, data privacy compliance was non-negotiable. Web inquiry forms could not store raw patient information on public form servers indefinitely, necessitating automated data-retention policies and controlled transfers into secure CRM databases.

2.3 Multi-Channel Patient Intake Across 7–8 Advertising & Web Sources

Prospective patients initiated contact through diverse touchpoints—including digital ad campaigns, hospital referrals, direct web portals, phone calls, and international outreach channels. Inbound inquiries entered through fragmented paths, creating triage delays and inconsistent preliminary screening.

2.4 Patient Verification & Clinical Record Control

Prosthetic customization requires verified medical documentation and eligibility checks before manufacturing and billing can commence. The business needed structured record verification stages to ensure only medically validated patient files advanced to invoicing and production release.

2.5 Multi-Country Finance & Online Payment Collection

Finance operations crossed multiple legal entities and currencies. Customer invoices required integrated online payment gateways (supporting credit cards, local digital wallets, and bank transfers across regions) with automated invoice status reconciliation upon payment confirmation.

2.6 Inventory & Customized Prosthetics Fulfilment

Prosthetic manufacturing requires precise stocking of raw materials, mechanical joints, brackets, and custom liners. Stockouts halted production, while field technicians lacked visibility into parts availability. The organization needed automated reorder alerts, purchase order workflows, and multi-carrier shipping integration.

2.7 Workforce, Education & Cross-Border Reporting

Human resources required unified employee records and statutory payroll across countries. Clinical procedures demanded standardized staff training curricula via learning management, while executive leadership lacked consolidated reporting across patient intake, revenue realization, and delivery cycle times.

Pillar 01 User Governance

Zoho One Environment & 200+ User Access Architecture

Centralized User Provisioning: Deployed the enterprise Zoho One directory, provisioning over 200 user accounts across Japan, India, the Philippines, and regional clinics.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Configured granular security profiles ensuring medical coordinators, accountants, warehouse staff, and technicians only access authorized data.
Regional Data Segregation: Established territory sharing rules that maintain regional clinic data boundaries while granting executive management consolidated visibility.
Pillar 02 Patient Verification

Zoho CRM & Patient Lifecycle Verification

Omni-Channel Lead Ingestion: Unified prospective patient inquiries from 7–8 sources (Google Ads, Facebook campaigns, web forms, hospital referrals, phone calls) into Zoho CRM.
Telephony Call & Clinical Screening: Integrated telephony logging to support structured intake conversations, capturing patient history, injury details, and treatment eligibility.
Verified Record Control: Implemented mandatory verification checkpoints before lead conversion, locking clinical files prior to financial estimate generation.
Pillar 03 Privacy & Compliance

Zoho Forms & Automated Patient Data Retention

Confidential Intake Forms: Configured secure web intake forms with conditional logic for patient registration, medical history, and fitting measurements.
Automated Data Retention & Purge: Built scheduled data-retention workflows that automatically purge raw form responses from web servers after CRM synchronization.
Compliance Audit Trails: Preserved complete submission timestamps and transmission logs meeting healthcare privacy standards across regions.
Pillar 04 Finance & Invoicing

Zoho Books Invoicing & Online Payment Workflows

CRM-to-Books Handoff: When clinical verification completes, CRM deal details automatically trigger customized tax invoices in Zoho Books in local currencies.
Integrated Payment Gateways: Connected Stripe, Razorpay, and regional payment providers, enabling patients to pay securely via credit card, net banking, or local wallets.
Automated Payment Reconciliation: Real-time webhook notifications mark invoices as Paid in Zoho Books and notify clinical coordinators to release the prosthetic order.
Pillar 05 Supply Chain & Fulfilment

Zoho Inventory Management & Prosthetics Fulfilment

Component Tracking & Assembly BOM: Structured inventory items for prosthetic joints, sockets, feet, and adaptors, tracking batch numbers and serialized components.
Automated Reorder Points: Set min-max thresholds per component, triggering automated purchase orders to medical suppliers before stockouts impact assembly schedules.
Installation Kits & Dispatch Tracking: Tracked mobile fitting kits allocated to field prosthetists and integrated courier tracking APIs for patient parcel tracking.
Pillar 06 Workforce & Operations

Global Workforce, Knowledge & Executive Intelligence

HRMS & Statutory Payroll (People & Payroll): Unified employee master records, leave management, and localized payroll disbursement across international entities.
Recruitment & Medical Training (Recruit & Learn): Structured applicant tracking for clinical specialists and deployed Zoho Learn for ongoing prosthetics calibration training.
Executive Dashboards (Zoho Analytics): Built cross-departmental KPI dashboards monitoring patient intake velocity, clinical verification ratios, and fulfillment turnaround times.
