01 / Overview

Executive Project Overview

The client is an innovative global MedTech enterprise utilizing state-of-the-art 3D-CAD modeling, industrial 3D printing, and machine learning algorithms to produce affordable, high-precision custom prosthetics and orthotics for patients worldwide.

With medical teams and regional operations distributed across Japan, India, and the Philippines, the organization required a cohesive, enterprise-grade Zoho One architecture to manage multi-channel patient intake, virtual clinical fittings, digital healthcare agreements, strict medical compliance, and multi-currency billing reconciliation across 200+ international users.

02 / Bottlenecks

The Regulatory & Operational Challenges

Medical Data Compliance

Healthcare privacy laws across Japan and global territories required strict role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure clinical data and scans are securely restricted.

Data Tampering & Fabrication Errors

Once biometric measurements were certified by prosthetists, there was no locking mechanism to prevent accidental field edits prior to 3D printing.

Disconnected Invoicing & Currencies

Handing off verified orders to finance was manual, causing delays in multi-currency deposit collections (JPY, INR, PHP, USD) and bank reconciliations.

Multi-Country Telehealth & HR

Managing virtual consultations across time zones, digital e-signed consent waivers, and 200+ staff across 3 countries lacked a unified platform.

03 / Solution

Core Architectural Solution Pillars

Pillar 01 Patient Intake & Security

Compliant Patient Intake & Immutable Verification Gate

Encrypted Patient Ingestion: Captured medical inquiries and hospital referrals via secure Zoho Forms directly into Zoho CRM with field-level encryption for sensitive health parameters.
Immutable Record Locking Gate: Built an automated Deluge verification gate. Once clinical coordinators certify patient scans, the record enters an immutable locked state, preventing edits and preserving CAD manufacturing fidelity.
Pillar 02 Finance & Multi-Currency

Automated CRM to Zoho Books Multi-Currency Billing Pipeline

Automated Patient Account Provisioning: Upon record locking, custom Deluge webhooks automatically push treatment plans and product specs into Zoho Books.
Live Bank Feeds & Reconciliation: Connected international corporate bank accounts in Japan, India, and the Philippines for automated multi-currency transaction matching.
Pillar 03 Legal, Telehealth & HRMS

Digital Consent (Zoho Sign/Contracts) & Multi-Country HRMS (Zoho People)

Automated Legal Contracts & Telehealth: Dispatched multilingual medical waivers with biometric e-signatures via Zoho Sign and embedded virtual fittings via Zoho Meeting.
Centralized Multi-Country HRMS: Deployed Zoho People across Japan, India, and Philippines for 200+ employees, managing localized holiday calendars, shifts, and leave approvals.
